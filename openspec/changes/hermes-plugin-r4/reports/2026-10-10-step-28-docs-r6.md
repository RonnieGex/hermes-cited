R5 M-R5-5 asked for the documented promise to match the implemented policy, and D11 asked the proposal to say
what the host would have to offer to activate the seal.

## Result

Every document names the retention, the single capability point and the scope of the row. The fixtures of
`tests/regressions/test_r5_messages.py` read the text the registered handlers return, not the words of a
document: the handler of `/cited` contains `no se emite`, `mensaje final` and `borradores`, and the handler of
`/dot` contains `cualquier respuesta`, `entidades` and `enlaces`.

State before/after: before, `/cited` promised "su fuente y su sello" and `/dot` promised that no answer "se
sella ni se reemplaza"; after, both state the implemented policy and the retention.
