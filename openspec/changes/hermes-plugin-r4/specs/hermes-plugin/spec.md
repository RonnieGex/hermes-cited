## MODIFIED Requirements

### Requirement: H4 — Two modes: the model chooses and the plugin writes the cited answer

The plugin SHALL register `/cited` and `/dot`, changing the mode of the conversation, and SHALL read the
initial mode from `CITED_DEFAULT_MODE`, defaulting to `dot`. In cited mode the plugin SHALL inject through
`pre_llm_call` the rules that make the model *choose* lines instead of writing the answer. The text the model
returns in cited mode SHALL be discarded whole and SHALL be replaced by the plugin's own render (H12); the
model's prose SHALL never reach the user in cited mode. A deterministic, non-AI verifier SHALL assert by
construction that every displayed block is identical to a full line of a non-empty `passages[].excerpt` or
`citations[].excerpt` of that same turn, with no Unicode normalisation, no substring comparison and no word
lists. The evidence SHALL be only those excerpts: never the query, the generated `cited_ask` answer, a heading,
a position, a count or the transport text. With no selected line, with a `status: refused` anywhere in the
turn, or with zero excerpts, the answer SHALL be the fixed refusal of H12 and SHALL NOT carry the seal. A
refusal of Cited SHALL block every selection of that turn, whether the selection was made before or after the
refusal (H13). Citation marks `[n]` SHALL be generated by the plugin from the unique passage identifier of the
turn and SHALL NOT be read from the model's text (H14). The seal `Verified by Cited` SHALL be written only by
the plugin, only after the exact delivered text was persisted, and only when the delivery policy of the surface
allows it **and** the host accredits the delivery of the turn (H16); otherwise the answer SHALL say that the
seal was withheld. In dot mode the model's text SHALL be
delivered intact without the seal: every occurrence of the mark SHALL be removed, in any presentation of the
supported consumer — Markdown markup, emphasis, inline and reference links, named and numeric entities and
soft line breaks included — and the text recorded in the log SHALL be the text delivered (H12). When the
removal cannot complete, the delivered text SHALL be a fixed safe note and never the model's untransformed
text or `None` (H16). The mark SHALL be detected in the
**visible text** of the admitted Markdown presentations, including internal emphasis (`Verified **by** Cited`)
and link labels (`Verified by [Cited](url)`); dot mode SHALL remove those occurrences whole and cited mode SHALL
withhold the seal with the fixed note. The visible text SHALL be the one the **same Markdown consumer the host
uses** produces (`markdown_it` of the Hermes installation): the document is rendered, every tag is replaced by
a space, every entity is decoded and the whitespace is collapsed, so an entity inside a link label, a shortcut
reference resolved against its definition and the line break an HTML `<br>` renders are one mark (M-R6-1). The
removal SHALL map every visible character back to the span of the original document that produced it, so the
whole construct goes with the mark; the postcondition SHALL be checked **on that render**, not on the detector
again, and a removal that cannot complete after a bounded number of passes SHALL deliver the fixed safe note
and never the model's untransformed text. A document name or a section SHALL have every interpretable Markdown
metacharacter escaped, not only the backslash and the backtick, so it can never inject a link, an emphasis or a
body. The plugin SHALL NOT raise into Hermes from any of its handlers (H11).

#### Scenario: the mode command changes the mode
- **WHEN** `/cited` runs
- **THEN** the next turn SHALL be cited, and after `/dot` the next turn SHALL be dot again

#### Scenario: the model's prose is discarded
- **WHEN** the final text of a cited-mode turn states a datum in its own words, changes a value, moves a subject or writes the reserved seal
- **THEN** none of that text SHALL be delivered, and the answer SHALL be the render of the lines the model chose or the fixed refusal

#### Scenario: only a full line of an excerpt is displayed
- **WHEN** a cited-mode turn displays a block
- **THEN** that block SHALL be identical, byte for byte, to a full line of an excerpt of the same turn

#### Scenario: no selection is a fixed refusal
- **WHEN** the model calls no `cited_quote`, Cited refuses, or the turn has no excerpt
- **THEN** the answer SHALL be the fixed refusal and SHALL NOT carry the seal

#### Scenario: a refusal blocks a later selection
- **WHEN** a Cited result of the turn carries `status: refused` and the model selects an excerpt of an earlier result of the same turn
- **THEN** the answer SHALL be the fixed refusal of the configured language and SHALL NOT carry the seal

#### Scenario: the seal is written by the plugin only
- **WHEN** the model's own text contains `Verified by Cited`
- **THEN** in cited mode it SHALL be discarded with the rest of the prose, and in dot mode every occurrence of it SHALL be removed, including `**Verified by Cited**`, `> Verified by Cited`, `Verified by Cited [1]`, `Verified **by** Cited`, `Verified by [Cited](https://example.invalid)`, `Verified\nby Cited`, `Verified &#98;y Cited` and `Verified by [Cited][c]`

#### Scenario: an entity, a reference and an HTML break do not hide the mark
- **WHEN** a model answer renders the reserved mark through an entity inside a link label (`Verified [&#98;y](https://example.invalid) Cited`), an entity inside the label of an inline link (`Verified by [Ci&#116;ed](https://example.invalid)`), a whole link whose label carries it (`[Verified &#98;y Cited](https://example.invalid)`), a shortcut reference resolved against its definition (`Verified by [Cited]` with `[Cited]: https://example.invalid`), an explicit reference with an entity (`Verified by [Ci&#116;ed][c]` with `[c]: https://example.invalid`) or an HTML line break (`Verified<br>by Cited`)
- **THEN** the detection SHALL read the render of the supported consumer, dot mode SHALL remove the whole occurrence and re-check the render, and cited mode SHALL keep the documentary line as data and add the fixed documentary note with `verified=false`

#### Scenario: a removal that cannot complete delivers the fixed safe note
- **WHEN** the render still shows the mark after the bounded removal passes
- **THEN** the delivered text SHALL be the fixed safe note, the row SHALL be that same text, and the model's untransformed text SHALL never be delivered

#### Scenario: document metadata cannot forge the seal
- **WHEN** a document name or a section of a passage contains the reserved mark, a line break or Markdown
- **THEN** the metadata SHALL be shown escaped in a single line, the seal SHALL be withheld and a fixed note SHALL be delivered in its place

#### Scenario: dot mode is untouched
- **WHEN** the mode is dot
- **THEN** the delivered text SHALL be the model's text without any seal, the log SHALL record that same text, and when removing the seal leaves nothing the delivered and recorded text SHALL be the fixed note

### Requirement: H5 — Local consistency record of delivered answers

The plugin SHALL append one entry per **plugin composition whose append succeeds** to
`$HERMES_HOME/cited/audit.jsonl` with: UTC
timestamp, a monotonic `seq` starting at 1, the identity of the log, platform, session, turn identifier, user
when available, mode, question, the tool calls with their arguments and with the SHA-256 and the text of every
returned excerpt, the unique identifier, original number, document, section and position of the lines the model
chose, the composed text, the transcript and the delivery decision, and the `prev_hash` and `hash` of the
chain, where `hash = sha256(prev_hash + canonical_json(entry without hash))`. A persistence refusal SHALL
produce no row, so the record is the plugin's composition when the append succeeds and never a claim about the
terminal message. Every entry SHALL also store the
evidence table of its turn: for each passage, the call, the identifier, the unique `uid`, the original number
`n`, the document, the section, the position and the numbered lines, so a block can be resolved independently of
the answer text. The append SHALL hold an exclusive inter-process lock over a lock file of the log and, before
writing one byte, SHALL read the whole chain and validate every schema, every `seq`, every link and every hash;
any mismatch SHALL refuse the append without modifying the log, and the append SHALL NOT restart from genesis
when the tail is invalid. The publication SHALL prepare the validated published bytes plus the new row in a
temporary file of the same directory, complete write, flush, `fsync` and close there, and only then replace the
published file atomically; a failure of any earlier stage SHALL leave the published bytes unchanged, and the
replacement SHALL be the last fallible step of publication. The plugin
SHALL NOT create or read any custody file: no key, no checkpoint and no file outside `HERMES_HOME`. When the
append fails, the delivered answer SHALL be the fixed operational refusal of H10 without a seal. A verifier
runnable as `python -m hermes_cited.verify_log <file>` SHALL check the internal consistency of the chain and
SHALL state that consistency is what it checked. It SHALL reject byte or link corruption: an entry altered,
an entry removed from the middle, an entry reordered, or an entry that does not carry the schema. A complete
suffix cut or a whole file rewritten with every hash recomputed SHALL stay internally consistent, and the
verifier SHALL NOT promise to detect either.

#### Scenario: the chain verifies
- **WHEN** `python -m hermes_cited.verify_log` runs on a log the plugin wrote
- **THEN** it SHALL report the number of entries and its internal consistency, and exit zero

#### Scenario: tampering is detected
- **WHEN** an entry is altered, removed from the middle or reordered
- **THEN** the verifier SHALL exit non-zero and SHALL name the first broken entry

#### Scenario: a complete suffix cut stays consistent
- **WHEN** the last complete entries are cut from the end of the log, or the whole file is rewritten with every hash recomputed
- **THEN** the remaining chain SHALL still be internally consistent and the verifier SHALL exit zero, because internal consistency is the only thing it checks

#### Scenario: an alteration never authenticates a later append
- **WHEN** a log is altered and a legitimate append follows
- **THEN** the append SHALL be refused and the log SHALL be unchanged

#### Scenario: a failed append never reaches the published chain
- **WHEN** preparing the temporary file fails at write, flush, `fsync`, close or the replacement
- **THEN** the published bytes SHALL be identical to the ones before the attempt, no row SHALL be accepted, and the next append SHALL be accepted

#### Scenario: a block resolves against the evidence table
- **WHEN** a block of a delivered answer is checked
- **THEN** its call, identifier, `uid`, document, section, position and line SHALL be the ones of a row of the entry's evidence table, and the whole block SHALL appear in the answer

#### Scenario: no custody file is written
- **WHEN** the plugin appends an entry under any configuration
- **THEN** no file outside `HERMES_HOME` SHALL be created or read for that append, and `CITED_AUDIT_KEY_PATH` SHALL have no effect

### Requirement: H9 — The delivery policy is a snapshot of the surfaces that can be seen

The plugin SHALL decide the seal from a snapshot of the effective delivery configuration taken at the **start**
of the cited turn, not from a re-read of the configuration at the end, and SHALL record that snapshot, its
`source` and its decision in the append. The policy SHALL come from the configuration object the host itself
loaded (`hermes_cli.config.load_config_readonly`) when this process carries an explicit `HERMES_HOME` and the
host's own config path is the one of the home being asked about; a standalone run SHALL read the file. When the
host module is present and does not answer with a mapping, the snapshot SHALL be unreadable and the seal SHALL
be withheld. A `FailedConfigRead` or any object that carries a `read_error` SHALL be rejected as unreadable,
never accepted as the last valid mapping. A YAML syntax or type error SHALL be an unreadable snapshot; the
absence of a YAML parser SHALL be
distinguished from an invalid document and SHALL NOT reinterpret the error as a permissive configuration. The
seal SHALL be withheld on `api_server` and on `desktop`; on any messaging platform,
and on the CLI, when streaming is on for that surface or when interim commentary is on for it; and whenever
streaming TTS is enabled. On a platform the plugin cannot determine, and when the snapshot cannot be read, the
seal SHALL be withheld. The seal SHALL be granted only when the snapshot affirmatively shows that nothing else
could have been visible **and** the host accredits the delivery of the turn (H16); while the host does not
expose that contract, the seal SHALL be withheld on every route, including a quiet configuration the plugin can
read. The plugin SHALL NOT promise that no draft reached the screen: the promise of H12 is about the final
message.

#### Scenario: the desktop SSE route withholds
- **WHEN** a cited turn runs with `platform="api_server"`
- **THEN** the answer SHALL be the render without the seal and SHALL carry the fixed note that names that surface

#### Scenario: streaming withholds
- **WHEN** streaming is enabled for the platform of the turn
- **THEN** the seal SHALL be withheld and the note SHALL say what to turn off

#### Scenario: interim commentary withholds
- **WHEN** interim assistant messages are enabled for the platform of the turn
- **THEN** the seal SHALL be withheld

#### Scenario: streaming TTS withholds
- **WHEN** `tts.auto_tts` is enabled
- **THEN** the seal SHALL be withheld

#### Scenario: the snapshot does not follow a change
- **WHEN** the configuration changes after the turn started
- **THEN** the decision of that turn SHALL stay the one taken from the snapshot at its start

#### Scenario: an unreadable configuration withholds
- **WHEN** the configuration cannot be read, or its YAML has a syntax or type error
- **THEN** the seal SHALL be withheld and the answer SHALL be the render with its note

#### Scenario: the loaded host policy beats the file
- **WHEN** the object the host loaded enables commentary or TTS while the configuration file disables it
- **THEN** the snapshot SHALL follow the loaded object, SHALL name `source: host` and SHALL withhold the seal

#### Scenario: a host read error is not the last valid configuration
- **WHEN** the host loader answers with a `FailedConfigRead` or any object that carries a `read_error` while the file is quiet
- **THEN** the snapshot SHALL be unreadable and the seal SHALL be withheld

#### Scenario: the seal waits for the host
- **WHEN** every surface the snapshot can read is quiet, because the host exposes no per-turn accredited delivery
- **THEN** the answer SHALL be the plugin's composition with the fixed note and SHALL NOT carry the seal

### Requirement: H10 — A failure never yields a sealed answer, and the plugin's composition is one of three

For every cited turn that reaches `transform_llm_output`, the plugin's composition SHALL be exactly one of: the render
of H12, the fixed refusal of H12 in the configured language, or a fixed operational refusal in the configured
language that carries an incident code and interpolates no error detail. This requirement bounds that
composition and not the terminal message: the host may add its own text after the hook, so the delivered message
can differ from it (H16). A boundary around the whole
transformer SHALL turn any exception of reading the configuration, resolving, rendering, composing or
persisting into the operational refusal, and SHALL drop the turn state in `finally`. The mode SHALL start
**unknown**: the dot pass-through SHALL be allowed only after dot was resolved affirmatively, so a failure of
the resolution can never return the model's prose. The incident code SHALL be generated at the failure site and
SHALL never raise: when the entropy dependency fails, a constant non-empty reserve code SHALL be returned
instead, and the whole error path down to the localized refusal SHALL depend on no entropy source, no disk, no
configuration and no logger. The diagnostic SHALL be
redacted of the bearer — recursively, in the exception, its arguments and its chained cause — and SHALL go to
`stderr` and the logger only; no raw `exc_info` SHALL reach a logger. When the host does not call the hook at all
(the empty response of the real finalizer) the plugin SHALL declare the incompatibility and SHALL NOT present it
as covered.

#### Scenario: a failure anywhere produces the operational refusal
- **WHEN** reading the configuration, resolving the turn or the mode, rendering, composing or appending raises
- **THEN** the delivered text SHALL be the fixed operational refusal with an incident code, SHALL NOT carry the seal, and SHALL NOT contain the error text

#### Scenario: the refusal survives a failure of entropy
- **WHEN** the entropy dependency of the incident code raises while the append and the refusal are being built
- **THEN** the delivered text SHALL be the fixed operational refusal with the constant reserve code, SHALL NOT carry the seal, SHALL NOT contain the model's prose, and the turn SHALL be dropped

#### Scenario: the dot pass-through needs an affirmative resolution
- **WHEN** the mode cannot be resolved and no dot was recorded for the turn
- **THEN** the answer SHALL be the operational refusal and never the model's prose

#### Scenario: the turn state is dropped
- **WHEN** the transformer returns, with or without an exception
- **THEN** the turn state of that turn SHALL no longer be retained

#### Scenario: an operational refusal does not carry the mark
- **WHEN** the error message or any metadata contains the reserved mark
- **THEN** the delivered operational refusal SHALL contain no occurrence of it

## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: H13 — A Cited refusal blocks every selection of the turn

The turn SHALL record every refusal a `cited_ask` result carries through `post_tool_call`. When the turn holds
at least one refusal, `transform_llm_output` SHALL deliver the fixed refusal of the configured language with no
seal, and SHALL NOT render any line, whether the selection was recorded before or after the refusal. This
SHALL hold in the full path (`pre_llm_call` → tool calls → `cited_quote` → `transform_llm_output`).

#### Scenario: a selection before the refusal is not rendered
- **WHEN** the model selects a line and a later `cited_ask` of the same turn returns `status: refused`
- **THEN** the answer SHALL be the fixed refusal and SHALL NOT carry the seal

#### Scenario: a selection after the refusal is not rendered
- **WHEN** a `cited_ask` of the turn returns `status: refused` and the model then selects a line of an earlier passage
- **THEN** the answer SHALL be the fixed refusal and SHALL NOT carry the seal

### Requirement: H14 — One identifier per passage, bound to the turn of the execution

Each passage a cited tool returns in a turn SHALL receive a unique identifier inside that turn, and the turn
SHALL keep the mapping of that identifier to the call, the original number `n` of the Cited result, the
document, the section and the position. The identifier SHALL be what the model passes to `cited_quote`, what the
render shows as its `[n]` mark and what the append records. `cited_quote` SHALL bind the selection to the turn
of the execution identity the host provided; when more than one turn of the session is active and the turn
cannot be told apart, the selection SHALL be rejected instead of assigned to the last one. A numeric `passage`
SHALL be accepted only while it identifies exactly one passage of the turn; when it is ambiguous the selection
SHALL be rejected with an error that asks for the identifier. Two passages of the same turn that carry the same
original `n` SHALL both be selectable through their identifiers. The identifier SHALL be assigned while the
search or ask result is built, bound to the turn that the execution identity resolves, and the annotated result
SHALL be exactly what the model receives; the hook SHALL read that same record and SHALL NOT renumber it. Only
the real `cited_search` and `cited_ask` handlers SHALL feed the evidence of the turn, the envelope's own
`cited_tool` SHALL match the handler that produced it, and a `cited_quote` result SHALL be recorded as a
selection and SHALL NEVER be reparsed as evidence.

#### Scenario: two searches of one turn do not collide
- **WHEN** two searches of the same turn return a passage with the same original number and the model selects the second one by its identifier
- **THEN** the render SHALL display the line of the second passage with the identifier the model used

#### Scenario: an ambiguous numeric passage is rejected
- **WHEN** the model passes a numeric `passage` that matches more than one passage of the turn
- **THEN** the selection SHALL be rejected, nothing SHALL be recorded, and the error SHALL ask for the identifier

#### Scenario: an ambiguous turn is rejected
- **WHEN** two turns of one session are active and a selection cannot be bound to one of them
- **THEN** the selection SHALL be rejected and nothing SHALL be recorded

#### Scenario: the model receives the identifier it must pass
- **WHEN** the model calls `cited_search` twice in one turn through the registered handler
- **THEN** each result SHALL carry its own `pid`, and that same `pid` SHALL be accepted as the `passage` argument of `cited_quote`

#### Scenario: a quoted envelope is never evidence
- **WHEN** a document line happens to be a valid Cited envelope and the model quotes it and its result reaches `post_tool_call`
- **THEN** no new passage SHALL be created and no document or text of that line SHALL appear as a second source

#### Scenario: every input type is safe
- **WHEN** `cited_quote` receives a boolean, a float, a negative number, zero, a superscript digit, a five-thousand-digit string or an out-of-range index as `passage` or inside `lines`
- **THEN** it SHALL return the fixed error sentence, SHALL record nothing and SHALL NOT raise into Hermes

### Requirement: H15 — The host incompatibilities are declared with their integration evidence

The plugin SHALL carry an integration test that runs the real host finalizer
(`agent.turn_finalizer.apply_llm_output_transform`) and the real hook dispatcher with a temporary `HERMES_HOME`,
and SHALL record in the evidence: the empty response never reaches the hook; an exception of the transformer
does not reach the caller; the delivered text of a cited turn is one of the three of H10; and the real finalizer
can append its file-mutation footer after the transformer and after the append, so the persisted row can differ
from the final message. The README and `docs/upstream-proposal.md` SHALL state the three contracts the host does
not offer: synchronous retention of the
delivery (text, reasoning, commentary, voice and the SSE route), a final composition step even when the
model's response is empty, and a cited composition executed after all the host footers and before the terminal
delivery. The plugin SHALL NOT open a pull request or an issue in the upstream repository.

#### Scenario: the empty response is recorded as an incompatibility
- **WHEN** the integration test calls `apply_llm_output_transform` of the host with an empty response in a cited turn
- **THEN** the host SHALL return the empty text without invoking the hook, and the plugin SHALL report that as an incompatibility instead of covering it

#### Scenario: the exception is contained
- **WHEN** the configuration file is unreadable during a cited transform of the real dispatcher
- **THEN** the delivered text SHALL be the fixed operational refusal without a seal and the turn SHALL be dropped

#### Scenario: the host footer is a declared limit
- **WHEN** the real finalizer appends its file-mutation footer after the transformer persisted the render
- **THEN** the integration test SHALL record that the persisted row is the plugin's composition without the footer and without a seal, that the final message carries the footer, and that this limit is declared instead of covered by C1

### Requirement: H16 — The seal waits for an accreditable host (D11 of the round 6 mission)

The mark `Verified by Cited` SHALL NOT be emitted on any route while the host does not expose an accreditable
delivery contract: a per-turn policy of the effective receivers (voice, commentary, streaming and the SSE route)
plus the guarantee that the terminal message is the plugin's composition. The plugin SHALL keep **one capability
point** that answers whether the host accredits the delivery of the turn; with Hermes v0.21.6+387 (`dce1e9b`)
it SHALL answer "not accredited" on every route, and a future host version would activate it there, only with a
test of the real host that demonstrates the contract. The snapshot SHALL record the surfaces it can read
(`surface_clear`) and the accreditation separately, and the seal SHALL require both. The answer SHALL carry the
fixed withheld note that names the missing accreditation. The record SHALL state in words that the row registers
the **plugin's composition**, that the host may show drafts while the answer is generated and add its own notices
at the end, and that the row is therefore not a record of the terminal message. A document or a section of a
passage that carries the reserved mark SHALL still be shown as escaped data, never as state. This requirement
amends C1 to C4 of the round 4 contract as recorded in the round 6 mission and in D17 of the design.

#### Scenario: no route emits the seal
- **WHEN** a cited turn is delivered on a quiet CLI, on a quiet messaging platform or on any other surface
- **THEN** the answer SHALL NOT contain the mark, SHALL carry the fixed note that names the unaccredited delivery, and the entry SHALL record `verified: false` and `seal_withheld: true`

#### Scenario: the capability point is the only blocker
- **WHEN** the capability point is turned on and every surface the snapshot can read is quiet
- **THEN** the same fixture SHALL emit the seal, proving that the absence of the host contract is what withholds it

#### Scenario: the record names its own scope
- **WHEN** an entry is read
- **THEN** it SHALL carry the fixed sentence that says the row registers the plugin's composition and is not a record of the terminal message

#### Scenario: the reserved mark is data, not state
- **WHEN** a document name, a section or a chosen line carries the reserved mark
- **THEN** the metadata SHALL be escaped including the entity ampersand, the line SHALL keep its bytes as data, the seal SHALL be withheld and the fixed note SHALL be delivered

### Requirement: H17 — The installed skill and the public documents state the implemented policy (M-R6-2)

The skill the plugin registers with `ctx.register_skill` SHALL state that this version **does not emit** the seal
`Verified by Cited`, SHALL show a real render that carries the fixed retention note instead of a render that ends
in the mark, and SHALL describe the plugin's composition, the drafts the host may show while the answer is
written, the empty response and the notices the host may add afterwards. The public documents — both READMEs, the
packaged skill, `docs/upstream-proposal.md` and `community-hermes/docs/mcp.md` — SHALL replace every universal
promise about the terminal message or the record with: *for a cited turn that reaches the hook, the plugin's
composition is the documentary render, the fixed refusal or the operational refusal; the host may add text
afterwards*. They SHALL describe the JSONL as the record of that composition when the append succeeds, so a
persistence refusal produces no row, and dot mode as the model's text with the mark removed and a fixed note when
the removal leaves nothing. The number and the names of the three pending host-side contracts SHALL be aligned in
every one of them. A test SHALL read the skill the plugin really registers and SHALL search the public documents
for the old promises with a list of **forbidden** phrases; checking that the new phrases appear SHALL NOT be the
only assertion.

#### Scenario: the registered skill declares the retained seal
- **WHEN** the skill the plugin registers is read
- **THEN** it SHALL say that this version does not emit the seal, SHALL carry no render that ends in the mark, and SHALL name the plugin's composition, the drafts, the empty response and the additions of the host

#### Scenario: an old promise cannot survive in a public document
- **WHEN** the public documents are searched for the forbidden phrases of the previous contract
- **THEN** no document SHALL contain one, and each SHALL carry the replacement wording and the three pending contracts by name
