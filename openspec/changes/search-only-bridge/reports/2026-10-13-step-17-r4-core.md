## 13.2 (D6/M01-R3) and M01-B2R2 / M02-B2R2 — the open defects of this round

The bridge2 round 2 review executed the pinned host's real dispatcher and left five open defects. The pinned
`model_tools._execute_tool` dispatches `task_id` and `session_id` but never the turn (`model_tools.py:826`),
while the host composes its turn identifier as `"<session>:<task>:<suffix>"` (`agent/turn_context.py:586`).

`cited_quote` refused a normal full turn. The same resolver also stripped the identity before matching, let an
unmatched `turn_id` fall through to the live turn its `task_id` named (R01-B2R2), and the observer fabricated
an identity-less second execution or revived a completed turn.

The five independent probes of `tasks/test_bridge2_codex_r2.py` are ported as permanent regressions in
`tests/test_bridge2_amendments.py`.
