The intended failures are the H19-H22 contract that the implementation does not satisfy yet:

- `test_profile_scope.py` — `config.bridge_mode_of`, `config.bind_scope`, `config.active_scope`,
  `config.Registration`, `config.search_settings_for` and `config.CitedScopeError` do not exist
  (`AttributeError`), including the concurrent-scopes case.
- `test_search_only_mode.py` — search registration does not exist, so the unknown-mode case registers the
  full surface and the handler cases fail.
- `test_search_wire.py` — `tools.search_only`, `tools.SEARCH_WIRE_SCHEMA` and
  `config.chassis_endpoint_of` do not exist.
- `test_manifest_modes.py` — `plugin.yaml` is still 0.1.0 and still requires `CITED_MCP_TOKEN`.
- `test_profile_install.py` / `test_docs_bridge.py` — the READMEs and `docs/technical-manual.md` do not
  document the search mode yet.

Two defects of the tests themselves were found by this run and corrected before the GREEN step: the
manifest reader did not treat `python_dependencies: []` as a list, and the temporary spec copy has no
`H0` (the change adds H1-H22, so the loop starts at H1).

The one skip is the deliberate `test_a_fresh_real_profile_installs_the_search_bridge`: the disposable VM
of step 5.3 is not part of this run, and the test records that reason instead of faking the install.
