## MODIFIED Requirements

### Requirement: H4 — Two modes: the model chooses and the plugin writes the cited answer

The plugin SHALL register `/cited` and `/dot`, changing the mode of the conversation, and SHALL read the initial mode from `CITED_DEFAULT_MODE`, defaulting to `dot`. In cited mode the plugin SHALL inject through `pre_llm_call` the rules that make the model *choose* lines instead of writing the answer. The text the model returns in cited mode SHALL be discarded whole and SHALL be replaced by the plugin's own render (H12); the model's prose SHALL never reach the user in cited mode. A deterministic, non-AI verifier SHALL assert by construction that every displayed block is identical to a full line of a non-empty `passages[].excerpt` or `citations[].excerpt` of that same turn, with no Unicode normalisation, no substring comparison and no word lists. The evidence SHALL be only those excerpts: never the query, the generated `cited_ask` answer, a heading, a position, a count or the transport text. With no selected line, with a `status: refused`, or with zero excerpts, the answer SHALL be the fixed refusal of H12 and SHALL NOT carry the seal. Citation marks `[n]` SHALL be generated by the plugin from the passage number and SHALL NOT be read from the model's text. The seal `Verified by Cited` SHALL be written only by the plugin, only after the exact delivered text was persisted, and only when the delivery policy of the surface allows it; otherwise the answer SHALL say that the seal was withheld. In dot mode the model's text SHALL be delivered intact and SHALL never carry the seal: a seal written by the model SHALL be removed.

#### Scenario: the mode command changes the mode
- **WHEN** `/cited` runs
- **THEN** the next turn SHALL be cited, and after `/dot` the next turn SHALL be dot again

#### Scenario: the model's prose is discarded
- **WHEN** the final text of a cited-mode turn states a datum in its own words, changes a value, moves a subject or writes the reserved seal
- **THEN** none of that text SHALL be delivered, and the answer SHALL be the render of the lines the model chose or the fixed refusal

#### Scenario: only a full line of an excerpt is displayed
- **WHEN** a cited-mode turn displays a block
- **THEN** that block SHALL be identical, byte for byte, to a full line of an excerpt of the same turn

#### Scenario: no selection is a fixed refusal
- **WHEN** the model calls no `cited_quote`, Cited refuses, or the turn has no excerpt
- **THEN** the answer SHALL be the fixed refusal and SHALL NOT carry the seal

#### Scenario: the seal is written by the plugin only
- **WHEN** the model's own text contains `Verified by Cited`
- **THEN** in cited mode it SHALL be discarded with the rest of the prose, and in dot mode it SHALL be removed from the delivered text

#### Scenario: dot mode is untouched
- **WHEN** the mode is dot
- **THEN** the delivered text SHALL be the model's text without any seal, and no seal SHALL be added

### Requirement: H5 — Auditable hash-chained log that fails closed

The plugin SHALL append one entry per response to `$HERMES_HOME/cited/audit.jsonl` with: UTC timestamp, a monotonic `seq` starting at 1, the identity of the log, platform, session, turn identifier, user when available, mode, question, the tool calls with their arguments and with the SHA-256 and the text of every returned excerpt, the lines the model chose, the final response, the verifier result, and the `prev_hash` and `hash` of the chain. The append SHALL hold an exclusive inter-process lock over a lock file of the log and, before writing one byte, SHALL read the whole chain, validate every schema, every `seq`, every link and every hash, SHALL authenticate the checkpoint against the key, and SHALL cross the result with a high-water mark per identity that lives next to the key, outside `HERMES_HOME`. Any mismatch SHALL refuse the append without modifying the log or the checkpoint; the append SHALL NOT restart from genesis when the tail is invalid. Genesis SHALL be allowed only for a new identity registered in custody, never because a file disappeared. The key SHALL be created before the first row and SHALL never be created or changed by a read; a lost key for a registered identity SHALL fail closed and require explicit recovery. The append SHALL be a recoverable transaction between the log and the checkpoint, so the recorded row is the response that was delivered. `CITED_AUDIT_KEY_PATH` inside `HERMES_HOME` SHALL be rejected. A verifier runnable as `python -m hermes_cited.verify_log <file>` SHALL detect an altered, removed, reordered or cut entry, a rewritten and recomputed chain, a rolled-back or missing checkpoint, and a log below its high-water mark.

#### Scenario: the chain verifies
- **WHEN** `python -m hermes_cited.verify_log` runs on a log the plugin wrote
- **THEN** it SHALL report the number of entries and exit zero

#### Scenario: tampering is detected
- **WHEN** an entry is altered, removed or reordered
- **THEN** the verifier SHALL exit non-zero and SHALL name the first broken entry

#### Scenario: an alteration never authenticates a later append
- **WHEN** a log is altered and a legitimate append follows
- **THEN** the append SHALL be refused and the log and checkpoint SHALL be unchanged

#### Scenario: genesis needs custody
- **WHEN** a log is emptied or deleted while its identity is registered in custody
- **THEN** the append SHALL be refused instead of starting a new chain

#### Scenario: a lost key fails closed
- **WHEN** the key of a registered identity is missing
- **THEN** a read SHALL NOT create a key, the append SHALL be refused, and the verifier SHALL exit non-zero

#### Scenario: a failure leaves the delivered row
- **WHEN** the checkpoint cannot be written after the row
- **THEN** the row SHALL be rolled back so that no response that was not delivered is recorded, and the answered text SHALL be an operational refusal without a seal

#### Scenario: the key never lives inside the home
- **WHEN** `CITED_AUDIT_KEY_PATH` points inside `HERMES_HOME`
- **THEN** the plugin SHALL refuse to use it

### Requirement: H9 — The delivery policy is the host's own resolution

The plugin SHALL resolve the effective streaming of a surface with the host's real `gateway.config.StreamingConfig` (its `from_dict` and `enabled_for`, including `mode`, `enabled`, `transport` and `display.platforms.<platform>.streaming`), and SHALL use a compatibility copy versioned against the supported host only when that import fails. The CLI without streaming SHALL be the only explicit exception that receives the seal; the CLI with its streaming on SHALL withhold it. The desktop app SHALL withhold the seal and carry the D1 warning that a draft may be visible. A messaging surface whose effective streaming is on SHALL withhold the seal and say so; a platform the plugin does not know SHALL NOT receive the seal by default, and SHALL follow the host's master switch. The READMEs SHALL document the exact configuration for the gateway, the CLI consumer rule and the desktop limitation.

#### Scenario: a messaging surface with streaming on withholds the seal
- **WHEN** the effective streaming for the platform is on, the mode is cited and the answer is rendered
- **THEN** the final text SHALL NOT carry `Verified by Cited` and SHALL say that the seal was withheld because streaming is on

#### Scenario: the host's mode alias is honoured
- **WHEN** the configuration is `streaming: {mode: edit}` or `streaming: {enabled: true, transport: false}`
- **THEN** the plugin SHALL match the host's own `enabled_for` result for the platform

#### Scenario: an unknown messaging platform is not exempt
- **WHEN** the platform is not one the plugin enumerates and the global switch is on
- **THEN** the seal SHALL be withheld

#### Scenario: the desktop limitation is documented
- **WHEN** a README is read
- **THEN** it SHALL say that the desktop app may show the draft while the answer is written and that the seal is withheld there

### Requirement: H10 — A persistence failure never yields a sealed answer

The plugin SHALL keep the session and turn identifiers of a turn, write them in the audit entry, and treat the audit append as a transaction that can fail. In cited mode, a failure to persist SHALL produce an operational refusal without the seal and a redacted diagnostic, the entry SHALL NOT survive as a row that was never delivered, and the turn state SHALL be discarded only after the append result is known.

#### Scenario: the log cannot be written
- **WHEN** the audit append raises a permission or disk error
- **THEN** the cited-mode answer SHALL be a refusal that says it could not be recorded, SHALL NOT carry the seal and SHALL report the verifier as not applied

#### Scenario: the session is recorded
- **WHEN** the hooks carry a `session_id` and a `turn_id`
- **THEN** both SHALL appear in the audit entry

## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: H11 — `cited_quote`: the model chooses numbered lines

The plugin SHALL register the tool `cited_quote` with the arguments `{passage: <number of a passage or citation of this turn>, lines: [<line numbers>]}`. Each passage or citation an envelope of the turn returned SHALL be split into lines deterministically by line breaks, where a paragraph with no line break is one line, and SHALL be numbered from 1 within its passage; the numbering SHALL travel to the model with the tool result. The handler SHALL validate that the passage exists in the turn and that every line number exists, SHALL return to the model the exact text it chose, and SHALL record the selection, in order and without duplicates, on the turn. An invalid selection SHALL return a sentence and SHALL record nothing. The handler SHALL never raise into Hermes.

#### Scenario: the model chooses a line
- **WHEN** `cited_quote` is called with a passage and line numbers of the turn
- **THEN** it SHALL return the exact text of those lines and the turn SHALL record the selection

#### Scenario: an unknown passage or line is refused
- **WHEN** the passage or a line number does not belong to the turn
- **THEN** the tool SHALL return a sentence naming the problem and the turn SHALL record nothing

#### Scenario: line numbers are deterministic
- **WHEN** the same excerpt is split twice
- **THEN** the lines and their numbers SHALL be identical, and a paragraph without line breaks SHALL be one line

### Requirement: H12 — The plugin writes the cited answer

In cited mode the plugin SHALL build the delivered answer itself and SHALL NOT include any text produced by the model. The render SHALL be: a literal entry template in the language of `CITED_LANGUAGE` (`es` by default, `en` when configured); then, for every chosen line, the line copied byte for byte from the excerpt of the turn, its `[n]` mark and its source (`document` and section) generated by the plugin; then, when the delivery policy allows, the seal `Verified by Cited`. When there is nothing to show the render SHALL be exactly the fixed refusal `No está en los documentos.` (Spanish) or `It is not in the documents.` (English). The verifier SHALL assert that every rendered block is identical to a full line of a turn excerpt.

#### Scenario: a real render carries line, source and mark
- **WHEN** the model selects a line of a passage
- **THEN** the answer SHALL contain that line byte for byte, the document and section of the passage, and the `[n]` mark, followed by the seal when the surface allows it

#### Scenario: nothing to show is the fixed refusal
- **WHEN** no line was selected, Cited refused or there was no excerpt
- **THEN** the answer SHALL be exactly the fixed refusal of the configured language and SHALL NOT carry the seal

#### Scenario: the language is configured
- **WHEN** `CITED_LANGUAGE` is `en`
- **THEN** the entry template and the refusal SHALL be the English ones, and the lines SHALL still be copied verbatim
