# Why

Round 2 of `hermes-plugin` closed with `GATE: GREEN` (38 of 38 claims), but the second independent
adversarial review of Codex (`tasks/revision-codex-hermes-cited-r2.md`, verdict **CAMBIOS**) found three
Blockers, five Majors and one Minor. Two rounds proved that verifying the model's free prose always
leaves a gap: one repair against two, zero against five, `-380` against `380`, `380` against `1380`, the
subject moved to the line above, the seal written by the model itself.

The mission `tasks/mision-cited-hermes-r3.md` fixes the resolution with the design decisions D5 to D8.
**D5 replaces D2**: in cited mode the model's text never reaches the user. The model only *chooses*
lines through a new `cited_quote` tool, and the plugin *writes* the answer from those lines.

# What Changes

- **D5 (B-R2-1, B-R2-2, M-R2-2).** Cited mode stops verifying prose and replaces it. Each passage
  `cited_search` or `cited_ask` returned in the turn is split deterministically into numbered lines (by
  line breaks; a paragraph with no break is one line). The plugin registers `cited_quote` with
  `{passage, lines}` and returns the exact chosen text to the model. In `transform_llm_output` the model's
  response is **discarded whole** and replaced by a fixed render: a literal entry template in the
  configured language (`CITED_LANGUAGE`, `es` by default), the chosen lines copied **byte for byte**,
  each with its source (document and section) and its `[n]` mark generated by the plugin, and the seal
  `Verified by Cited`, which only the plugin writes. Nothing to show — no `cited_quote` call, a Cited
  refusal or no excerpt — becomes a fixed-template refusal. The verifier is reduced to a check by
  construction: every displayed block is identical to a full line of a turn excerpt, with no Unicode
  normalisation, no substrings and no word lists. Dot mode is untouched: the model's text is delivered as
  it is, the seal is stripped if the model writes it, and it is never added.
- **D6 (B-R2-3, M-R2-3).** The audit log fails closed. Under the lock it validates the whole chain and
  authenticates the checkpoint **before writing one byte**; a mismatch is neither an append nor a seal.
  Genesis is only for a new identity registered in custody. A high-water mark per identity lives next to
  the key, outside `HERMES_HOME`. The key is created before the first row and never on a read; a lost key
  fails closed and needs explicit recovery. A recoverable transaction between the log and the checkpoint
  makes the recorded row the response that was actually delivered. `CITED_AUDIT_KEY_PATH` inside
  `HERMES_HOME` is rejected. The READMEs state the residual scope: whoever controls the key folder too
  can roll everything back; remote custody arrives with Cited Ledger.
- **D7 (M-R2-1).** The delivery policy uses the host's real `gateway.config.StreamingConfig` and its
  resolution of `mode`, `enabled`, `transport` and per-platform overrides, with a versioned compatibility
  copy only when the import fails. The CLI without streaming is the only explicit seal exception; the
  desktop app keeps the D1 warning; a messaging surface the plugin does not know no longer receives the
  seal by default.
- **D8 (M-R2-4, M-R2-5, m-R2-1).** Exactly as the review proposes: the gate parses complete values with
  boundaries, sign, unit and a citation bound to the run's evidence; the refusal predicate follows the
  conservative contract and is independent of the detector it tests; the seal is crossed with the real
  verifier result and its audited entry; the raw-stream claim measures what its name says; every artifact
  is generated, redacted, scanned on disk, and only then is the verdict emitted; the task list, the state
  and the report counts/timings/tool order match their captures. **The reproductions of both reviews
  become gate and test fixtures.**

# Impact

`hermes-cited`: `hermes_cited/render.py` (new), the reduced `verifier.py`, `tools.py` (the third tool),
`plugin.py` (the render path), `audit.py` (custody), `delivery.py` (the host resolver), `config.py`
(`CITED_LANGUAGE`), `plugin.yaml`, the unit suite, `scripts/gate.py`, both READMEs, the packaged skill, and
the new OpenSpec change `hermes-plugin-r3` with its `reports/`.

`community-hermes` (worktree, branch `docs/hermes-agent`): documentation only in `docs/mcp.md`,
`README.md` and `README.es.md`, adjusted to the new cited mode.

No product code, no graphics, no landing, no deploy, no push, no archive of a change, and not one byte
written under `%LOCALAPPDATA%\hermes` or `~/.hermes`.
